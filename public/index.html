    QUESTIONS THAT MATTER

    Fit with confidence.

    Can this determine my exact size from a photograph?+

    No. An uncalibrated photograph cannot reliably determine real-world body dimensions. We intentionally use user-measured dimensions rather than pretending to calculate exact centimetres from images.

    Are these sample clothing sizes from real brands?+

    No. Demo Atelier is a fictional, illustrative catalog so visitors can test the system. For shopping decisions, supply and verify each real retailer's chart.

    How should I measure my body?+

    Use a flexible measuring tape, standing naturally. For chest, measure the fullest circumference; for waist, the natural waist; for hips, the fullest part; and for inseam, the inside leg from crotch to ankle. Keep the tape level and comfortable.

    Do you save or sell my measurements?+

    No. This demo does not include an account system, body-profile database, measurement analytics, or image uploads. Your measurement payload is processed by the API only to create the current recommendation.
